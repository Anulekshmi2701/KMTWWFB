Educational Scholarship Scheme

Discover our educational scholarship programs, designed to support and empower students of registered motor transport employees in pursuing their academic goals and dreams. Please carefully review the step-by-step instructions and document checklist below before submitting your online application.

Review the section-by-section requirements to complete the Educational Scholarship application accurately:

  • Active Registered Employee *: The parent must be an active registered employee of KMTWWFB with continuous, up-to-date monthly welfare fund contributions.
  • Employee Registration Number *: Enter your valid KMTWWFB Employee Registration ID and registered mobile number to verify your profile via OTP.
  • District Executive Office *: Confirm your registered District Office where your welfare subscription is maintained.

Student Profile:
  • Full Name of Student * (As per school/college records)
  • Date of Birth *, Gender *, and Aadhaar Number *
  • Relationship with Registered Employee * (Son / Daughter)
  • Previous Qualifying Examination Passed * & Percentage / Grade *
Eligible Courses:
  • Higher Secondary: Plus One (+1) & Plus Two (+2)
  • Undergraduate & PG: BA, BSc, BCom, MA, MSc, MCom
  • Professional & Technical: Engineering (B.Tech), Medical (MBBS/BDS/Nursing), Polytechnic Diploma, ITI, B.Ed

Institution Details:
  • Name & Full Address of School / College / University *
  • Current Academic Year & Year of Study *
  • Head of Institution Certification Date *
Bank Account Details:
  • Account Number *: Active personal savings account of the student or employee
  • IFSC Code *: Valid 11-character bank branch IFSC code
  • Bank & Branch Name *: Ensure Aadhaar seeding is active for smooth DBT credit

Important Note: Incomplete applications or blurred mark list uploads may be returned for correction by the District Executive Officer. Always preview all uploaded files before final submission.
  • Preview & Declaration: Verify all entered marks, bank account digits, and uploaded certificates before checking the declaration box.
  • Application Reference Number: Download and save the acknowledgment receipt generated after submission.
  • Status Tracking: Use the Application Status portal to monitor District Office approval and scholarship disbursement.
