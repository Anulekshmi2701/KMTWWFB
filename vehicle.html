Step-by-Step Vehicle Registration Order

Click each stage below to view the exact fields and instructions in sequential order:

  • Login: Log in to the Employer / Vehicle Portal using your registered credentials.
  • New User Registration: If you do not have a login account yet, complete the new user registration first and then log in.
  • Add Vehicle: Once logged in, select the "Add Vehicle" option from your dashboard.

The first page after clicking Add Vehicle is Form Two. Enter the vehicle and owner details here:

  • Vehicle Number *: Enter the vehicle registration number.
  • Type of Vehicle *: Select vehicle type.
  • Reg. Date *: Vehicle registration date.
  • Current Employer Reg. Date: Registration date under the current employer.
  • District *: Select the applicable district.
  • Name of Employer *: Tick "Same As Profile Owner" if applicable.
  • Address of Employer * & Pin Code *: Complete owner address.
  • Additional Details if any: Extra remarks (optional).
  • Form 2 Uploads: RC Book *, Permit, and Other Supporting Documents.
Note: If the profile creator is not the actual Employer of the vehicle, untick "Same as Profile Owner" and enter the actual Employer's name before submitting.

Form 3 is the dedicated page for entering the details of workers employed in the vehicle:

Worker Details Required in Form 3:
• Vehicle Number *
• Employee Name *
• Job Role *
• Employee Address
• Work Assigned From *
• Mobile Number
• Email ID
• Aadhaar Number
• Remarks
Important for Employers with Workers: After adding workers in Form 3, each worker must separately apply through the Employee Registration Page and pay ₹25 to complete their individual registration.

If the vehicle owner is self-employed (working on their own vehicle), tick the "Self Employment" checkbox and complete the Form 1 Application:

Personal & Local Body Details:
  • Name of Employee *, Father's Name *, Aadhaar Number *
  • Address *, Pincode *, DOB & Age *, Gender *
  • Mobile Number *, Email, Job Role *, Marital Status *
  • General & Technical Education, Service Commencement Date *
  • Local Body: District, Taluk *, Village *, Place *
Licence, Bank, Nominee & Family:
  • Licence: Licence No. *, Expiry Date *, Badge Available *, Badge No. & Expiry Date
  • Bank: Account No. *, IFSC Code *, Bank *, Branch *
  • Uploads: Licence *, RC Book *, Bank Passbook *, Ration Card *, Photo *, Aadhaar *, One & Same Certificate
  • Nominees & Family Profile: Nominee Name, Address with Pincode, Age, Relation, Percentage (100%) + Family Members table

"I hereby declare that the above-stated information is true to my knowledge, and I acknowledge that I will proceed with my application according to the rules and regulations set forth by the Kerala Motor Transport Workers Welfare Fund Board." Tick "I Agree"
  • For Self-Employed Applicants: Tick "I Agree" on the declaration, click Final Submit, and pay the ₹25 registration fee online to complete your registration.
  • For Employers with Workers (Form 3): After submitting Form 2 & Form 3, ensure each added worker logs into the Employee Registration module, submits their application, and pays the ₹25 fee.
