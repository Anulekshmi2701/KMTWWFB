Step-by-Step Payment Screen Explanation

Understand every field and calculation displayed on the Payment Services screen before making your transaction:

  • Separate Payment Pages: The portal provides two dedicated payment options — one for Employee Subscription Payment and another for Vehicle Contribution Payment.
  • Search Input: Enter your valid Employee Code (e.g., KMT/10/A/W/025615) on the Employee page, or your Vehicle Number (e.g., KL65B4673) on the Vehicle page.
  • Security Captcha: Type the displayed Captcha code accurately and submit to fetch your live account and payment ledger.

Once verified, the page displays your registered profile card with photo. Confirm that the following details belong to you before proceeding:

• Photo & Name: Registered worker/owner photo and full name
• Employee Code: Unique KMTWWFB worker ID
• Vehicle No & Code: Registration number & KMT vehicle code
• Employer Name & Mobile: Registered employer & contact number
• Vehicle Type & Table No: Category (e.g., Autorickshaw) & slab table
• Monthly Subscription: Fixed monthly rate & Subscription Paid Upto month

Below the profile summary, the Regular Payment section automatically calculates your pending dues:

  • Pending Pay From & To: Displays the exact unpaid duration (e.g., October 2021 to October 2026).
  • Exemption Details Table: Lists any government-approved exemption periods (Sl. No., From Period, To Period, Exemption Type).
  • Pending Contribution (Rs.): Total principal subscription due for the pending months.
  • Pending Interest (Rs.): Applicable late payment interest calculated automatically by the system.
  • Advance Months & Advance Amount (Optional): If you wish to pay in advance for upcoming months, enter the number of Advance Months; the Advance Amount will be calculated automatically.
  • Grand Total (Rs.): Final payable amount combining Pending Contribution + Pending Interest + Advance Amount.

At the bottom of the payment page, choose one of the authorized banking gateways to pay via UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, or Net Banking:

BOB Payment Gateway
Bank of Baroda (Supports all UPI, Cards & Net Banking)
SIB Payment Gateway
South Indian Bank (Supports all UPI, Cards & Net Banking)
Receipt Generation: Do not close or refresh the browser window during payment. Once successful, you will be redirected back to download/print your official KMTWWFB payment receipt.
