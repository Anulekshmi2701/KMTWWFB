About the Board

The Kerala Motor Transport Workers' Welfare Fund Board (KMTWWFB) is a statutory body functioning under the Department of Labour and Skills, Government of Kerala. It was established to ensure social security, financial stability, and welfare for workers employed in the private motor transport sector across the state.

Headquarters & Statewide Structure

The Head Office is located in Kollam, supported by 14 District Offices operating across Kerala, each headed by a District Executive Officer (DEO) to ensure accessible service delivery at the grassroots level.

Core Welfare Schemes
Pensions

Superannuation pension, disability pension, and family pension.

Financial & Medical Aid

Ex-gratia accidental death assistance, medical support & maternity benefits.

Family Support

Educational scholarships for children, daughter marriage aid & retirement benefits.

OUR JOURNEY & EVOLUTION

History & Key Milestones

From the landmark Act of 1985 to full-scale digital transformation and Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT).

June 27, 1985
1985 – Enactment & Inception (Act 21 of 1985)

To provide social security to unorganized motor transport workers (private bus, lorry, taxi, and auto-rickshaw staff) not covered under the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) Act, the Government of Kerala enacted the Kerala Motor Transport Workers' Welfare Fund Act, 1985, which came into force on June 27, 1985.

July 11, 1985
Launch of the Welfare Fund Scheme

Following the enactment of the Act, the Kerala Motor Transport Workers' Welfare Fund Scheme was officially framed and implemented on July 11, 1985, formally establishing the Board to administer the welfare fund across Kerala.

2005 Amendment
2005 – Major Amendment & Self-Employed Inclusion

Initially, coverage was limited to traditional employer-employee setups. Through the Amendment Act of 2005 (Act 23 of 2005), the scope was significantly expanded to bring self-employed motor transport workers (owner-cum-drivers of autos, taxis, and goods vehicles) under the welfare net.

Statutory Linkage
Motor Vehicle Tax Linkage

The 2005 amendment introduced a crucial mandate requiring vehicle owners to produce a Welfare Fund Contribution Clearance Certificate while paying Motor Vehicle Tax, leading to massive growth in active membership and long-term fund sustainability.

Digital Era
Modernization & Digitalization

Over the past decade, the Board transitioned from manual record-keeping to a centralized online portal, streamlining member registration, e-payment of contributions, instant clearance certificates, and Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT) for pensions and educational scholarships.
