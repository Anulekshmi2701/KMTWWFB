ABOUT THE BOARD

Dedicated to the Welfare & Security of Kerala’s Motor Workers.

Established by the Government of Kerala, the Kerala Motor Transport Workers Welfare Fund Board (KMTWWFB) is committed to providing comprehensive social security and financial support to motor transport workers across the state. From retirement pensions and medical aid to educational assistance for workers' children, we ensure a dignified and secure future for every registered driver, conductor, and transport employee.

Welfare Benefits

Schemes & Online Services for Motor Transport Workers

The Kerala Motor Transport Workers Welfare Fund Board (KMTWWFB) works for the welfare of workers in Kerala's motor transport sector. Under the Kerala Motor Workers' Welfare Fund Act, in force since 1985, the Board provides marriage, maternity, medical, death, pension, scholarship, funeral and other welfare benefits.
