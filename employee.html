Employee Registration Application

Application form collects essential personal and employment details from prospective employees, including vehicle information if applicable, and allows for the upload of necessary documents such as identity proofs. Please carefully read the instructions and review the required documents listed below before submitting your online employee registration application.

Form Sections & Information Required

Review the section-by-section details required to complete the online registration form:

  • Vehicle Number *: Select the registered vehicle number where the employee is currently working.
  • Employee (As per Form 3) *: Select the employee's name as submitted by the employer in Form 3.
  • Date of Commencement of Service *: Enter the exact date on which the employee joined service in the vehicle.

Personal Details:
  • Name of Employee *, Aadhaar Number *, Father's Name *
  • Address of Employee *, Pincode *, Date of Birth & Age *
  • Gender *, Mobile Number *, Registered District *
  • Marital Status *, Job Role *
  • General Education, Technical Education & Email ID (Optional)
Local Body Details:
  • District: Select your district of permanent residence
  • Taluk *: Select the corresponding Taluk
  • Village *: Select your Village
  • Panchayat / Municipality / Corporation *: Choose your applicable Local Self-Government body

Licence & Badge Details:
  • Licence Number * & Name as on Licence *
  • Licence Expiry Date *
  • Is Badge Available * (If Yes, provide Badge Number and Badge Expiry Date)
Bank Details:
  • Account Number *: Active personal bank account number of the employee
  • IFSC Code *: Valid 11-character bank branch IFSC code
  • Bank & Branch *: Name of the Bank and Branch

Nomination Clause: In the event of my death before becoming eligible for or receiving the welfare fund amount due to me, I hereby nominate the listed person(s) to receive the said amount in the specified share proportions.
  • Nominee Details: Nominee Name *, Address with Pincode *, Age *, Relation *, and Share Percentage * (Total must equal 100%).
  • Employee Family Profile: Add family members with Sl. No., Name, Gender, DOB & Age, Relation with Member, Job, Remarks, and Aadhaar Number.
  • Declaration: Confirm that all stated information is true to your knowledge and agree to abide by the Board's rules and regulations before submitting.
