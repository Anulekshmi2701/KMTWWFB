What You Can View on the Status Page

Once you submit your Application Number / Reference ID, the portal displays full transparency across four key tracking parameters:

  • Where the File Is Now: Identifies the exact KMTWWFB District Office or Head Office currently holding your file.
  • Current Processing Seat: Shows the exact administrative desk/seat level (e.g., Clerk / Junior Assistant Seat, Senior Superintendent, District Executive Officer (DEO), or Chief Executive Officer (CEO)) where your application is awaiting verification or approval.

Pending / Under Verification: File is currently being reviewed at the assigned seat.
Approved / Sanctioned: Application verified and approved; ID or benefit processed.
Returned for Correction: Sent back due to missing/unclear documents (check Remarks).
Rejected: Application declined with official reason listed in Remarks.

  • Full Application Preview: View all personal, vehicle, employer, bank, nominee, and scheme claim details submitted in your application form.
  • Submission & Movement History: Check the exact date of submission, payment transaction reference (if applicable), and movement timestamps between office seats.
  • Print / Download Acknowledgment: Download or print your application status summary for future office reference.
